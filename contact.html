Quick Questions

Can I request a new calculator?

Yes! Select "Calculator Feature Request" in the dropdown menu. We prioritize models backed by popular user demand.

Is there an API?

Not currently. Our calculator engines calculate parameters locally on client browsers. We have no backend API endpoints available.

Can I embed your calculators?

No direct iframe embed is supported, but you are completely welcome to link back to any calculator page. Review our Terms of Use.
