CAGR Formula and Context

The Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) computes the smoothed annual yield rate needed for your start value to compound up to your end value over a specific horizon:

CAGR = (End Value / Start Value)1/n - 1

For stock portfolios, comparing expected returns against standard indexes like the S&P 500 provides baseline metrics. Historically, the S&P 500 index has compounded at approximately 10.7% annually over the last 30 years (with dividends reinvested), setting a prominent growth benchmark for global wealth generators.
